#  Supermarket Sales Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes 9,800+ supermarket sales transactions to uncover
customer behavior, purchasing patterns, sales trends, and geographic
revenue drivers.

The analysis goes beyond simple sales totals to identify different
customer groups and translate the findings into actionable business
recommendations.

---

##  Project Objective

The main objective is to understand:

- Who are our customers?
- How do different customer groups spend?
- What products do they purchase?
- Which customer segments drive revenue?
- Which cities generate the most revenue?
- What sales patterns and seasonal trends exist?
- How can businesses use these insights to sell smarter?

---

##  Analysis Process

### 1. Data Preparation
Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
Explored customer behavior, sales patterns, product categories,
geographic performance, and purchasing trends.

### 3. Customer Segmentation
Identified two major customer groups:

- Everyday Buyers
- High-Value Buyers

### 4. Visualization
Created charts to communicate important patterns and findings.

### 5. Business Recommendations
Translated analytical findings into practical recommendations
for marketing, inventory, and sales strategies.

---

##  Key Findings

### Customer Segmentation

The analysis identified two distinct customer groups:

- **Everyday Buyers:** approximately 88% of transactions
- **High-Value Buyers:** approximately 12% of transactions

Everyday Buyers make smaller, regular purchases, while High-Value
Buyers make larger and less frequent purchases.

---

###  Purchasing Behavior

Everyday Buyers primarily purchase basic products such as:

- Binders
- Paper
- Art

High-Value Buyers show stronger purchasing patterns around:

- Chairs
- Phones
- Tables

---

### Sales Trends

The analysis identified different sales behaviors between the two
customer groups.

Everyday Buyers provide relatively predictable purchasing activity,
while High-Value Buyers contribute to significant sales spikes,
particularly during Q4.

---

###  Geographic Analysis

Sales are concentrated in several major cities.

New York City and Los Angeles were identified as important markets,
with strong relationships to high-value purchasing behavior.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

### 1. Customer-Specific Marketing

Different customer groups should receive different marketing
strategies instead of receiving the same offers.

### 2. Q4 Budget Campaign

Target High-Value and Corporate customers during the Q4 budget season
with products such as chairs, phones, and tables.

### 3. Location-Based Strategy

Use high-performing cities and their product preferences to optimize
inventory and create targeted marketing campaigns.
